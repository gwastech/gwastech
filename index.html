The Origin

I TURNED MY CHILDHOOD DREAM INTO REAL ROBOTS

When I was a kid in Poland in the 90s, I watched a cartoon about a magical pencil—anything you drew became real. I wanted that more than anything, but everyone told me it was impossible.

Years later, that pencil turned into 3D CAD modeling, 3D printers, and Arduino code. EDDiE, WiNNiE, MiKE, and GWX-1 all began as drawings in my mind. Now they walk, talk, and move in the real world.
